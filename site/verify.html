Is it right? Check our numbers.

Control: stars that should not move

Known asteroids found blindly

We searched 24 ecliptic fields without being told where to look, then matched what we found to the known asteroids (SkyBoT, positions moved to where SPHEREx was). 217 known asteroids recovered: V<17 47 of 53, V<18 101 of 132. Median position offset 0.68″. 47 objects were linked into tracks; their speed agrees with the orbit prediction to a median 0.36 %. All asteroid results.
