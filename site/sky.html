SPHEREx sky blink

Pick any point of the sky. This page finds the SPHEREx exposures that cover it, reads only the needed rows of each image from the public archive on AWS (HTTP Range), and builds an aligned blink of the observing seasons in your browser. Moving stars jump; everything else stays still.

Examples: Barnard's Star Epsilon Indi B WISE 0855−0714 RW Aurigae R Draconis Luhman 16AB (coordinates)

SPHEREx data: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA), read from IRSA's public bucket on the Registry of Open Data on AWS. Not endorsed by NASA. Names: CDS Sesame / SIMBAD. Reference stars: Gaia DR3 (ESA, the European Space Agency) and Tycho-2 via CDS VizieR.
