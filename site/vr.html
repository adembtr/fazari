Dots: Gaia DR3 stars faster than 0.5″ per year. Colour = speed: 0.5–1″ 1–2″ 2–4″ over 4″ per year

Each dot slides at its Gaia DR3 proper motion. Radial motion and slow changes of proper motion are ignored, so time stops at ±2,000 years.

How it is computed: each star moves along a great circle of the sky at its Gaia DR3 proper motion (east and north parts, with cos δ). Ignored: motion toward or away from us, and the slow change of proper motion that it causes over tens of thousands of years. At ±2,000 years this puts the worst cases, Kapteyn's and Barnard's Star, up to about 15% off their shown shift; most stars much less. The background picture does not move: it is the sky as SPHEREx saw it in 2025–2026.
